damaris /// 2006 // 5. Semester
This website gathers projects, studies, and experiments from recent years. Conceived as an archive, it is intended to be continuously expanded over time. It reflects my love for collecting, curating, and revisiting ideas.
Driven by observation my work as an explorative designer seeks to challenge what is given. I value unconventional approaches and remain attentive to the contexts in which design operates in. A central focus is the connection between analog and digital media, with materiality playing a important role. I am eager to expand my knowledge, and my practice evolves through researching, testing, and sharpening ideas, both formally and conceptually.
I am especially drawn to interdisciplinary approaches, seeing design as a space for inquiry while exploring language and words creatively.
InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere Pro, After Effects, HTML/CSS, Figma, German, English, French
